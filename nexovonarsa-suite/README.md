# NEXOVONARSA WhatsApp Business Suite

Modular WhatsApp customer-service and business-management layer for Nararya Studio, Nararya Garage, Hilekros Products and Nararya Store.

Features: linked-device QR login, human-style Indonesian replies, catalog/order routing, purchase receipt generation, admin-only group approval, automatic group close at 23:00 WIB and reopen at 05:00 WIB, SQLite records, AES-256-GCM encryption, Excel/Word reports, Google Drive replace-in-place helper, responsive dashboard, TypeScript/JavaScript/Python/C++/CSS.

Security: do not collect KTP, KK, SIM, student cards or face images unless necessary and lawful. Encrypt sensitive files, restrict access, define retention/deletion, and never commit them. WhatsApp OTP is never captured by this app. Account linking uses WhatsApp linked devices.
